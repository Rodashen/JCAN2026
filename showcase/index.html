Does this include online payment processing?

The existing system lets the school confirm payment before approving exam access. Automated checkout is a separate feature to discuss.

Can a student share their account?

Email-bound codes and one active session at a time discourage casual sharing. A new sign-in ends the previous device’s session. Deliberate sharing cannot be completely prevented.

Is this an official HRD Korea exam platform?

No. It is an independent practice website for preparation. Official exam procedures and announcements come from the relevant authorities. Content, permissions, and attribution for another school’s deployment should be agreed before launch.

What would we need to get started?

Your school name and logo, course and schedule details, photos you have permission to use, and a list of the features you want. The scope and price can then be discussed directly.
