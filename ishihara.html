Color-vision practice

Ishihara practice

Each attempt has 12 randomized dot plates with numbers from 1 to 99. Retrying selects different numbers and creates new dot patterns. Use normal lighting, turn off night mode and color filters, and view each plate without zooming. Enter the number you see, or choose “I cannot see a number.” Answers appear after submission.

These original JCAN generated plates are inspired by Ishihara dot patterns; they are not validated diagnostic plates or a calibrated Ishihara examination. Screen colors affect results. This practice cannot diagnose color blindness or determine EPS eligibility; consult an eye-care professional for an official assessment.
