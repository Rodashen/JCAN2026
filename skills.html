How to prepare / Paano maghanda

  1. Recognise each picture and say its Korean name aloud. / Kilalanin ang larawan at sabihin ang Korean na pangalan.
  2. Study safety vocabulary, explain why a rule matters, then answer what you would do. / Aralin ang salita, dahilan ng safety rule, at tamang gagawin.
  3. Use the practice-topic selector below for new tools or one safety stage. / Piliin sa ibaba ang bagong tools o isang safety stage.

The manual has three difficulty levels (① easiest, ③ hardest) and allows emphasis on level ① for E-9 interviews. The Upper/Middle/Lower tool groups below retain the memo labels; they are not student scores.

Official references and practice guidance

HRD Korea lists self-introduction and attitude, basic conversation and actions, tool use and following instructions, vocational competency, and safety awareness as interview areas. Manufacturing skills include pin insertion and tasks such as joint connection, measuring and cutting, pipe bolting, grinder use, packaging, and pallet loading. Tasks can be selected randomly; follow the current announcement for your test.
